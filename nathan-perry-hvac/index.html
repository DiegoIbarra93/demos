Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about heating and cooling in the Rogue Valley.

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

We recommend preventive maintenance twice a year: once in spring for cooling and once in fall for heating. Regular tune-ups help prevent breakdowns, keep efficiency high, and extend the life of your equipment.

What size HVAC system do I need?

System size depends on your home's square footage, insulation, window efficiency, and ductwork. We perform a load calculation before recommending a replacement so you get the right capacity for comfort and efficiency.

Do you offer emergency HVAC repair?

Yes. When your furnace fails on a cold night or your AC stops during a summer heat wave, call us for prompt emergency service across Medford and the Rogue Valley.

Are heat pumps a good choice in Southern Oregon?

Heat pumps are an excellent fit for our climate. They provide efficient heating in winter and cooling in summer, often with lower operating costs than traditional furnaces and AC units.
