Systems & efficiency

Ducted or ductless, we'll find the fit

Not every space needs the same system. We compare equipment options, efficiency goals, and rebate opportunities so you can choose with confidence.

Ducted systems

Central furnaces, heat pumps, and air conditioners that distribute conditioned air through existing ductwork. Best for whole-home comfort.

  • Central furnace & AC
  • Air-source heat pumps
  • Ductwork assessment
Ask about ducted options

Ductless mini-splits

Room-by-room heating and cooling without ductwork. Ideal for additions, older homes, garages, or targeted comfort zones.

  • Single & multi-zone setups
  • Mitsubishi Diamond Contractor expertise
  • Year-round heating & cooling
