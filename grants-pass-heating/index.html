Ducted or ductless?

Two common ways to move conditioned air. The right fit depends on your building, your rooms, and what you already have.

Ducted systems

A central unit heats or cools air and sends it through ducts to every room. Often the best fit when ducts are already in place and in good shape.

  • Even airflow through existing vents
  • Single thermostat control
  • Works with furnaces, heat pumps, and AC

Ductless mini-splits

Wall- or ceiling-mounted units heat and cool individual zones. Useful for additions, converted spaces, or homes without ductwork.

  • Room-by-room temperature control
  • No duct installation or repair needed
  • Compact outdoor unit with quiet indoor heads

What to gather before asking

  • Approximate age of your current system
  • The problem or goal: repair, replace, new install, or maintenance
  • Square footage and number of rooms or zones
  • Any rooms that always run too hot or too cold
  • Recent energy bills, if you are comparing options
