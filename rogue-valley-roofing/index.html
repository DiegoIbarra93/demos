Roofing Services

Find the right route for your project

Whether you are protecting a home, a commercial building, or an industrial facility, choose the path that matches your scope.

Roof replacement

Full tear-off and re-roofing for aging or storm-damaged homes. Material options, ventilation review, and debris removal handled end to end.

New construction roofing

Installation planning for custom homes and developments across the Rogue Valley, from submittals to final inspection walkthroughs.

Roof inspections

Condition assessments for resale, insurance documentation, or peace of mind. Photos and a clear scope of recommended next steps.

Flat & low-slope systems

TPO, modified bitumen, and built-up roofing for offices, retail, and multi-tenant buildings. Emphasis on drainage and warranty compatibility.

Roof coatings & restoration

Extend the life of a sound commercial roof with coating systems designed to reflect heat and reduce thermal movement.

Maintenance programs

Scheduled inspections, gutter clearing, and minor repairs to help commercial properties avoid costly emergency work.

Industrial re-roofs

Large-scale removals and replacements for warehouses, manufacturing, and agricultural facilities with tight production windows.

Metal roofing

Standing seam and exposed-fastener metal systems for durability and low lifecycle costs on industrial buildings.

Safety & compliance

Project planning that respects site rules, OSHA considerations, and your operational schedule.

Leak repair

Targeted diagnostics and repairs for active leaks, flashing failures, and penetration issues on any roof type.

Gutters & downspouts

Installation, repair, and cleaning to keep water moving away from foundations, siding, and landscaping.

Skylights & sun tunnels

New installs, replacements, and leak remediation around skylights and light tubes.

Roof cleaning

Moss and debris removal that improves appearance and helps prevent moisture retention on shingle and tile roofs.

Storm damage checks

Post-weather inspections with photo documentation to support insurance conversations.

Preventive maintenance

Annual tune-ups that catch small issues before they become interior damage or major repairs.
