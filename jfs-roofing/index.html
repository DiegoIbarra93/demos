Straight Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions every roofing and framing customer asks — answered directly.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. We inspect the roof or framing scope in person, then provide a written, itemized proposal with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Oregon, and every project is backed by warranty-supported workmanship.

Do you handle both residential and commercial roofing?

Yes. We install and repair residential roofing — composition shingle and standing-seam metal — as well as flat and low-slope commercial roofing systems.

Do you do framing and new construction?

Yes. Framing is a core part of our work — custom homes, additions, shops, and agricultural buildings, from sill plate to ridge.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Jacksonville, Oregon and serve all of Southern Oregon, including Medford, Ashland, Grants Pass, the Applegate Valley, and Jackson and Josephine counties.

How long does a typical re-roof take?

Most residential re-roofs are completed in two to five working days, depending on size, pitch, and material. We confirm the schedule in writing before work begins.
