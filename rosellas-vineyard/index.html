Our Story

Rooted in one place, tended by one family

For decades, our vineyard has been shaped by the long summers and cool nights of the Rogue Valley. What began as a few acres of vines has grown into a working estate where every row is walked, every barrel is watched, and every bottle carries the character of a single harvest.

We believe wine should feel like an invitation—not a performance. Whether you are new to tasting or have a cellar of your own, our team pours with the same care we bring to the vineyard.

Frequently asked questions

Where are you located?

We are located in Grants Pass, Oregon, in the heart of the Rogue Valley wine country.

Reservations are recommended for groups of six or more. Walk-ins are welcome when space allows.
