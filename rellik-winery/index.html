Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need a reservation for a tasting?

Walk-ins are welcome for groups of six or fewer, Thursday through Sunday. Larger groups or prime weekend slots — reserve ahead and we'll set a table aside.

Where exactly is the tasting room?

In Central Point, Oregon, in the heart of Rogue Valley wine country — minutes from Medford and Jacksonville, right off I-5.

Can I get married or host a company event here?

Yes — weddings, rehearsal dinners, and corporate gatherings all work here, indoors or out. Send your date and headcount and we'll start planning.

Can I stay overnight?

Yes — the vineyard guest house sleeps small groups and sits steps from the tasting room. Stays book separately from tasting reservations.

Are kids and dogs welcome?

Kids, yes — with supervising adults. Dogs, yes — leashed, on the patio. Some evening events are 21+; the calendar marks them.
