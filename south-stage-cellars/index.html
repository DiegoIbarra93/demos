Taste Downtown Jacksonville

Our tasting room sits in a historic gold-rush town, open for walk-ins, reservations, and private groups.

Enjoy seated tastings, wine by the glass, and a rotating selection of small bites. Live music fills the patio on select Fridays, and the wine club picks up their allocations here each quarter.
