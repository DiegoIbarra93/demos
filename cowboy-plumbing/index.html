Our Services

Plumbing repairs, maintenance & replacements

From emergency repairs to planned upgrades, we handle the work that keeps your home or business running smoothly across the Rogue Valley.

Water Heater Repair & Installation

We repair and replace gas, electric, and tankless water heaters. If your unit is leaking, not heating, or past its life span, we will size and install the right replacement with proper venting and code-compliant connections.

Tankless Water Heater Installation

Tankless systems deliver endless hot water and lower energy bills. We handle gas-line sizing, venting, and on-demand unit mounting for Southern Oregon homes.

Service Line Repair

Low pressure, wet spots, or a spike in your water bill often point to a main line leak. We locate and repair service lines with minimal disruption to your Medford-area property.

Toilet Repair & Maintenance

Running toilets, weak flushes, clogs, and leaks waste water and money. We fix the symptom and the cause, or install a new toilet when repair is no longer cost-effective.

Frozen Pipe Protection

Rogue Valley winters can freeze exposed pipes fast. We insulate vulnerable lines, thaw frozen pipes safely, and repair burst pipe damage before it spreads.

Sink Repair

Kitchen and bathroom sink repairs, faucet replacements, drain cleaning, and leak detection. We stop drips, clear clogs, and restore proper drainage.

Tub & Shower Replacement

Upgrade worn tubs, showers, and valves with leak-free installations. We handle surround prep, valve rough-ins, trim, and fixture sealing.

Well Pressure Tank Installation

Fluctuating water pressure from a well usually means the pressure tank needs attention. We size, install, and troubleshoot well pressure tanks throughout the Rogue Valley.

Scheduled Maintenance

Annual inspections and preventive care catch small problems before they become expensive emergencies. Ask about our maintenance visits for Medford homes and rentals.
